Tonight at a glance

01
Format
Sealed, not draft. Nobody passes cards; you build only from your own Prerelease Pack.
The pack
6 Star Trek Play Boosters, 1 traditional foil promo, a deck box and a Spindown die (Wizards).
Your deck
At least 40 cards from your pool plus as many basic lands as you like. The store supplies basics. Everything you don't play is your sideboard.
Bring
Nothing is required. Sleeves, a pen and paper for life totals, and some dice for counters help.
Vibe
Wizards calls it "casual, friendly play." Ask your opponent or the staff whenever a rule is unclear.

Ask at check-in

  • How many rounds, and what time does the first one start?
  • Best-of-one or best-of-three games per round?
  • Can you swap sideboard cards in between games?
  • Are basic lands at the tables?
  • What are the prizes?

Build checklist

02
  1. Sort by color. Make one pile per color, plus piles for multicolor, colorless and lands.
  2. Find your two deepest colors. Count playable cards and removal in each. Wizards: "Your deck should include two of Magic's colors."
  3. Check the signposts. A gold or hybrid uncommon in your pair names its plan (see Archetypes). Build from what the pool actually holds.
  4. Pull removal first. Wizards: "aim to include at least five removal spells across your two colors."
  5. Find two or three threats in one of your colors, cards that win if left alone.
  6. Fill the curve with creatures. Use Wizards' target below for 23 spells.
  7. Add 17 lands, split by how many cards of each color you play.
  8. Cut situational cards and anything you'd be unhappy to draw.

Lands and splashing

  • Planet duals are common, one per color pair (Vulcan, Bajor, Qo'noS…). They enter tapped, gain you 1 life and tap for either color.
  • Anomaly lands (uncommon) tap for colorless and later sacrifice for an effect plus a New Planet token.
  • Fixing for a splash: Xenobotanist, Class-F Shuttlecraft, Warp Core, Unexplored Territory, Final Frontier and the Planets. Splash a third color only for a bomb or premium removal.
  • Basic landcycling {2} on Horta, Humpback Whales, Rogue Artificial Intelligence, Free Borg Revolutionaries and Cold-Blooded Crew turns a late-game body into a land early.

Mechanics

03
Spacecraft & Station
Spacecraft are artifacts. Station: tap another creature you control to put charge counters equal to its power on the ship. At the number shown (6+, 8+…) the ship becomes an artifact creature with the listed abilities.Station only at sorcery speed. The creature you tap stays tapped through your opponent's turn, so it can't block. A ship that just became a creature can attack only if you've controlled it since the turn began.
Beam me up
Cast the card again from your graveyard for its beam me up cost if you also return a creature you control to your hand. Then it's exiled.Sorceries are still sorcery speed. Returning a creature with an enter effect lets you reuse that effect.
Face a dilemma
You face a dilemma whenever you choose one or more modes for a spell or ability, so any "Choose one" card. Several cards reward it.The triggers resolve before the modal spell does, even if the spell is later countered.
Stun counters
If a permanent with a stun counter would untap, remove a counter instead. It's this set's main tempo tool.
Strange new worlds
Counts the differently named lands you control.Five Forests count as one. Planets, Anomalies, Unexplored Territory and New Planet tokens each add a name.
Federation
Counts creature types among your non-Borg creatures (Human, Vulcan, Officer…).
Landfall
Triggers whenever a land enters under your control, including from spells.
Total power 8
Red-white cards say "Whenever you attack while creatures you control have total power 8 or greater." That counts all your creatures, not just the attackers.
Also seen
Surveil, Investigate (Clue tokens), Kicker, Ward, Basic landcycling, finality counters (it's exiled if it would die). Assimilate shows up only on a few Borg rares.
Not in your packs
Battlefront and seek out appear on the Commander-deck cards, which guides say won't show up at the prerelease.

If it's a draft

09
  • Three packs each. Pack 1 passes left, pack 2 right, pack 3 left. Take one card and pass the rest without showing anyone.
  • Pick order: bombs, then removal, then evasive creatures, then efficient creatures, then filler.
  • Stay open for the first six or so picks. A color whose good cards keep arriving late in pack 1 is open.
  • Aim for about 23 playables plus 17 lands, the same build as above.
