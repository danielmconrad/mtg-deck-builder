How this list was made

Every Commander-legal card with a common or uncommon printing that has no copy in collections/brad, deck rows included, ranked by EDHREC popularity. Game Changers are left out, and so are cards whose cheapest printing costs over $5, which drops old "uncommons" priced like rares (Mana Drain, Lotus Petal, City of Brass) and Three Visits, just over at $5.07. Command Beacon is left out too, since its only uncommon printing is a promo, and so is Anguished Unmaking, whose only uncommon printing is in SIR, which has no paper price; every other printing is rare.

  • C common, U uncommon: the rarity of the printing linked, shown with its set code and collector number. Other printings of the same card may differ.
  • Prices are each card's cheapest printing in Scryfall's USD data, from the card cache refreshed 6 Nov 2026. Store prices will vary.
  • Card names link to Scryfall. Ticks are saved in this browser only.
